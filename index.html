About the Owner

I am the owner of Raven & Roses Cleaning Co.

We are a local, family owned small business, serving Nanaimo since February 2023.

At home, with my husband and our 2 boys, I run my own personal zoo — a large beautiful dog, 2 cats, and 3 turtles — so I know what a lived-in house really looks like.

A clean house helps with an organized mind — at least it is for me. That is why we do what we do: a tidy, calm home makes the rest of the week easier.

We are exclusively a Vancouver Island business, and we would love to grow to be able to provide services to many across Vancouver Island. We are excited to serve our community! We source our supplies and cleaners on Vancouver Island. We believe in shopping and supporting local.

We always use Pet, Kid & Eco-Friendly cleaning products because we also love our home, family and pets!
