How entries get here

When your employee clocks in, adds mileage, or sends a note in their app, it shows up here on its own. You don't have to type anything in or move anything over. Pick a date and an employee below, then tap Refresh to see their entries for that day.

Employees — codes, rates & access (Krystal only)

Each employee signs into their app with their name and their own personal code. Set the code here — tell it to them in person, never in a group chat or email thread. A code is blank until you set it; an employee with no code cannot sign in at all.

Revoking cuts off app access only. If you revoke someone, they cannot get into the app at all — even with the app still on their phone. Revoking never cuts off their own signed contract / information package: you give each employee their own paper copy AND their own digital copy (PDF) at signing, separately — never app-only. They always keep that, whatever happens to app access.

House instructions — edit (Krystal only)

Add what gets done at each house, plus special instructions. The employee sees this read-only in their app, by first name only. Do not put anything here you would not want the cleaner for that house to see.

This Month’s Focus — set the monthly tasks (Krystal only)

TO-DO in every house for a whole month, then it rotates — not a check or inspection. Set 1–2 tasks for the month (sometimes more, depending on the task and the season). They last the whole month so every house gets done properly, then next month you set the next task(s) here and it rotates. The employee sees these in their app under “This Month’s Focus” and ticks each one off per house.

How they get the update: this list is saved here. It shows up in their app when you save here, with no separate message needed.

Starter examples are loaded the first time only, clearly marked EXAMPLE — replace them with your real tasks. Examples: “Clean the vents” and “Vacuum under furniture”.

What to use — Supplies Guide — edit (Krystal only)

The employee sees this in their app as “What to use — Supplies Guide”, by the cleaning supplies. It is completely separate from House instructions above — house instructions say what to do in a specific house / room; this guide says what supply to use for an item / dirt, and how. Each item is clickable in their app: tap it and it shows a list of what it is good for, how to use it, and the notes — including the notes from the bottle / lid, so they do not have to go find the bottle to read it. They can open a room and search inside that room, or use the search bar at the top of the guide to search anything.

How they get the update: this guide is saved here. It shows up in their app when you save here.

Starter examples are loaded the first time only, clearly marked EXAMPLE — replace them with your real supplies, uses and label notes. Bar Keepers Friend is listed only if you confirm it is in the standard kit.

All guide items (tap Edit to change one, Remove to delete it)
