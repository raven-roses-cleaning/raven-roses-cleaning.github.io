Colours

  • No colour is chosen to start — tap a colour first, then colour. Until you do, touching the page just scrolls — nothing is drawn.
  • Basic colours — tap a big colour circle to use it straight away.
  • Colour wheel — tap the big rainbow box to pick any colour at all.
  • Colour schemes (Raven, Lilith, Sunset…) — ready-made palettes of colours that go well together, below the basic colours.
  • Try these for this page — colours picked to suit the page you're on. Just a starting point — use any colours you like.
  • Recent colours — colours you've used, so you can pick them again quickly.
  • Favourite colours — tap the heart, “Save as favourite,” next to the Current colour to keep a colour you love. It stays saved on this phone, ready in the Favourite colours row below. Tap a favourite to use it; tap its little × to remove just that one.

Tools

  • Fill — tap a light area once, quickly, to fill it. The black lines and blacked-out spots stay black. A swipe or drag scrolls instead, and never fills.
  • Pen — a firm, clean line. Pencil — softer and lighter. Brush — wide and soft-edged.
  • Blend — a very soft brush; go over an area more than once to build up gentle shading that melts into the colour next to it.
  • Eraser — drag over your colouring to rub it back to the plain line art.
  • Copy colour — tap any coloured spot on the page to copy that exact colour again, so you never have to hunt for it in the wheel. It becomes your colour straight away, and you land back on the tool you were using, ready to colour with it. It works even before you have chosen a colour.
  • Fill similar — tap a spot and every area coloured the same way is filled at once. Tap a white spot and all the still-white areas fill together; tap a coloured spot and every area in that colour changes together.
  • Fill all (under the tools) — fills every area that is still white with your current colour, in one tap.
  • Magnifier (under the tools) — turn it on and a round magnifying window follows your finger, so tiny details are easy to see. While it is on, the page cannot be coloured at all: dragging moves only the magnifier — the page stays still — and tapping does not fill or draw. Turn it off again to go back to colouring, and to scroll the page.
  • Undo & Redo (above Colours, just under Stroke size) — work for everything: fills, pen, pencil, brush, Blend, Eraser, Fill similar, Fill all, and Clear page. The line right under the buttons tells you what the last tap did.
  • Stroke size — how thick the Pen, Pencil, Brush, Blend and Eraser draw.
  • Fill tolerance — for Fill only. Slide left (Stricter) if fill leaks into the next area; slide right (Looser) if it won't fill a speckled area.
  • Double-tap the picture to enlarge it; double-tap again to go back. While enlarged, pinch to zoom and drag to move.
  • A quick straight swipe up or down on the picture scrolls the page instead of drawing.
