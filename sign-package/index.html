Welcome to the Team!

Welcome, we are excited you are joining our team of wonderful cleaners! We promote having fun and maintaining a great life / work balance. We want all our team members to feel included and valued.

About the Role

You are a Cleaning Technician with Raven & Roses Cleaning Co. You clean clients' homes to the Raven & Roses standard, mostly working solo in people's homes, following the schedule, route and checklist given for each clean.

  • Working solo: in people's homes, without check-ins during the job. You need to be comfortable working alone and trusted in a client's home.
  • Cats and dogs: in homes with pets. You need to be comfortable around cats and dogs.
  • Your own vehicle: to travel between clients' homes during your shift. See the Mileage section below for what travel is paid.
  • Energetic: and capable of constant movement while working. Cleaning is physical, active work for the whole shift.

Your Responsibilities — The Job Description

By accepting this job, you agree to all of the following. These are part of the job, not optional extras:

  • Clean every home to the Raven & Roses standard, every time — the same quality whether it is your first clean of the day or your last.
  • Follow the route and schedule given to you for each day. Go to the clients you are scheduled for, in the order and at the times given.
  • Follow the checklist for each clean.
  • Be reliable and on time. Arrival at each residence on time is expected. Your time for a clean starts when you arrive at the client's home.
  • Follow directions as given, and work independently without needing check-ins during the job.
  • Be hardworking, presentable and positive in attitude — in clients' homes and in any contact with clients.
  • Manage your time well during each clean so the full checklist is finished in the time booked.
  • Sign in and out on our own Raven time-card app for every job, and accurately track your mileage and your hours. See Our Apps and Mileage below.
  • Refill your supplies daily and look after them as described in the Supplies section below.
  • Protect client confidentiality at all times — see Trust, Confidentiality & Record Check below.
  • Tell Krystal promptly about any problem in a home — damage, something broken, a concern about a pet, or anything a client asks you to change.

If You Are Sick or Cannot Make a Shift

If you are sick, or for any reason you cannot make a shift you are scheduled for, you must do all of the following:

  • Inform the client, and make arrangements with the client — for example, agree a new time or day with them.
  • Inform Krystal as well, so she knows what has happened and what was arranged.
  • If you cannot make arrangements with the client, inform Krystal immediately so she can make other arrangements in time. Do not leave it.
Do not just not show up.

A no-show — not turning up for a scheduled clean without informing the client and Krystal as above — is not acceptable in this job.

Things You Are NOT Expected To Do

The following services are not provided by Raven & Roses Cleaning Co. and are not part of your job. If a client asks for any of these, do not do them — let Krystal know so she can respond to the client.

  • Dishes
  • Laundry
  • Carpet Cleaning (recommendation can be provided)
  • Cleaning Flat screen TVs
  • Exterior Windows (recommendation can be provided)
  • Garbage removal — no garbage removal except small garbages in bathrooms
  • Fireplaces
  • Animal waste
  • Hazard cleans
  • Cast Iron

In addition:

  • While cleaning, items will not be put away by Raven & Roses Cleaners unless otherwise agreed upon.
  • Raven & Roses Cleaners will not move furniture.

Hours & Shifts

  • Days: Part-time, Tuesday to Friday. No weekends are required.
  • Hours: 9am–12pm, or 9am–3pm / 9am–4pm — up to 24 hours per week.
  • Start of shift: Your time starts for the day when you arrive at the client's home for your cleaning shift.
  • Extra shifts: Opportunities to pick up extra shifts, such as move-out cleans on weekends, are available from time to time, but they are never a requirement.

Break Between Shifts — If It Is Wanted

This break only applies if you work both a morning shift and an afternoon shift on the same day, and only if the break is wanted that day.

  • When: There is a break between 12pm–1pm, between the morning and afternoon shifts.
  • Pay: The break is unpaid. You are not on the clock during it, and it is not paid time.
  • Your time: You are free to do as you choose during the break.
  • Back on time: You must arrive at your next job on time, ready to start, when the afternoon shift begins.

If you work only a morning shift, or only an afternoon shift, on a day, this break does not apply to that day.

  • Pay reviews: Pay is reviewed for increases based on performance. Reviews take place after the 3-month probation period and at least once annually after that. No increase amount is stated in this package — any increase is based on performance.

Mileage — Explained

Mileage is paid monthly, at the reasonable allowance rate set by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). It is paid for travel between clients only. Please read this carefully, because not all driving in your day is paid mileage:

Mileage IS paid for

  • Driving from one client's home to the next client's home on the same day — that is, travel between clients during your shift.

Mileage is NOT paid for

  • Driving from your own home to your first client of the day. That trip is not paid mileage.
  • Driving from your last client of the day back to your own home. That trip is not paid mileage.
  • Any personal driving, or driving during your unpaid break between shifts.
Example:

You drive from home to Client A (not paid), clean, then drive to Client B (paid — between clients), clean, then drive home (not paid). Only the Client A to Client B distance is claimed as mileage for that day.

  • Tracking: Record your between-clients mileage accurately for every day you work, and submit it as directed so it can be paid monthly. Only claim mileage that is actually between clients.
  • Submitting: Mileage must be submitted on the last day of the month.
  • Paid out: Mileage is paid out on the fifth (5th) of every month.
  • Hours: Accurately track your hours as well, using the time-card app for every job.

Supplies — All Provided For You

All supplies are provided by Raven & Roses Cleaning Co. Krystal provides the supplies and delivers them herself.

You never buy supplies.

Do not buy cleaning supplies, cloths or other work supplies for this job, and do not expect to be reimbursed for supplies you buy yourself — everything you need is provided.

  • Refill daily: Refill your supplies every day, for that day's jobs, from the stock Krystal provides and delivers. Refilling is part of your daily work, before / between jobs as needed.
  • The $1 / hour: The $1 / hour supply refill amount in your pay is pay for doing this daily refilling task. It is not a reimbursement for supplies you bought — you do not buy supplies.
  • Running low: Tap / record in the app, or otherwise note as directed, anything that is running low, so Krystal can restock it in time. Do not wait until you have run out completely.
  • Keep them in order: Keep your supplies in good order — bottles, cloths and kit kept clean, complete and ready for the next job.
  • Cloths: When cloths are actually washed, wash them with the soap provided for that purpose. Do not use your own products in place of the supplies provided.

Leaving — Returning Your Supplies

All cleaning supplies, tools and equipment provided by Raven & Roses Cleaning Co. remain company property. They are provided for your work at no cost to you. You must return all items on your last day of work (or by the return date we agree), and we will check them against the issue checklist you signed when they were given to you.

Final pay will be paid in full as required by law upon leaving our company, in accordance with BC regulations.

If any company property is not returned, we will contact you to arrange its return. If it cannot be returned, we may ask you to reimburse the replacement cost separately. Any such request will be dealt with apart from your pay.

Our Apps — Time Card & Safety

We use our own Raven & Roses apps. You will sign in and out at each residence, for each job, on our own time-card app, for safety and for time tracking. Use it for every clean, and record your supplies taps and mileage / hours as directed in this package.

There is no GPS tracking.

Uniform & Appearance

  • An apron will be provided.
  • Crocs: Provide your own Crocs for footwear, and clean them at the end of each shift.
  • No ripped jeans.
  • No short shorts.
  • No profanities on clothing.
  • Hair tied up.
  • No overly loose-fitting clothing — it can knock items over while you clean.

Trust, Confidentiality & Record Check

You work alone in people's homes, with their keys, codes and personal things around you. Trust is a core part of this job.

  • Confidentiality Agreement: You must sign the Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Agreement.
  • Criminal Record Check: You must provide a clean Criminal Record Check, completed after training and before working solo in clients' homes.
  • Protect client information: Protect all client information, including client addresses, home codes, keys, and any personal or private information you see or learn while working. Do not share it with anyone.
  • On leaving: On leaving Raven & Roses, return or delete all client notes, keys, codes, photos and lists in your possession, as set out in the Confidentiality Agreement.

What We Offer

  • Paid training — all training is paid.
  • Opportunities to advance — for team members who grow with us.
  • Emphasis on safety and wellbeing — in every home and on every shift.
  • Keep your tips — tips from clients are yours.
  • Bonus when possible — when the business is able to offer one.
  • Supportive work environment — a workplace where you are supported in the job.
  • Supplies for your own home: — you may use the supplies provided to clean your own home only, not other people's houses.
